# Built-in imagegen prompts

The following are the original prompts for the three notebook surfaces. Current use is documented in [README.md](README.md); the revised warm settings background has its own [exact prompt](settings-prompt.md). The main menu now uses the live harbor scene.

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: production 2D game UI panel background, portrait 3:4 composition, no typography.
Primary request: a beautifully restrained antique celestial sea-chart stationery panel for the main menu of a gentle island exploration game, inspired by the delicate watercolor storybook atmosphere of The Little Prince. The panel is flat and viewed perfectly from the front; subtle warm ivory cotton paper fills the entire rectangular image. Hand-painted very fine muted midnight-blue and antique brass curved navigation arcs, a small compass star and a few tiny golden stars are concentrated within the outermost 5 percent of the margins and bottom corner; a tiny distant sailboat and small round asteroid at the bottom edge. Central 85 percent must remain near-uniform pale ivory, light and clean for readable dark Chinese UI text. Uneven watercolor pigment and fine paper fibers, poetic quiet wonder, sophisticated sparse ornament, lots of empty space.
Constraints: exact rectangular edge-to-edge panel asset, no surroundings, no book mockup, no perspective, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no thick gold rectangular double border, no laurel corners, no baroque filigree, no UI buttons.

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: production game naturalist journal UI background, landscape 4:3, flat front view.
Primary request: an original delicate watercolor herbarium page from a poetic coastal exploration storybook with the quiet hand-painted mood of The Little Prince. Very pale cool sage-ivory watercolor cotton paper, extremely fine visible paper texture. Sparse translucent pressed sea grass, two tiny wildflowers, a subtle butterfly and a small shell inhabit only the outermost 3 to 5 percent of the edges, asymmetrical and hand-drawn rather than mirrored. A few tiny pencil specimen marks with absolutely no readable text. The central 90 percent must be empty, flat, bright, almost uniform ivory for dark UI text and specimen cards. Sparse botanical silhouettes, natural loose edges, refined muted sage and sandy beige. No frames of gold, no laurel corners, no compass, no book spine, no fold down the middle, no scenery, no labels, no letters, no words, no watermark, no buttons. Perfect rectangular edge-to-edge image to be used as a background texture.

Use case: illustration-story
Asset type: flat front-facing landscape 16:9 UI texture for a cozy game's settings and home-storage ledger.
Primary request: a refined little island workshop log sheet, hand-painted in gentle French watercolor children's storybook aesthetic. Very pale warm gray-blue linen paper, almost uniform empty central 94 percent. Only a thin faded terracotta pencil line near the outermost bottom and top edge, a tiny hand-drawn bronze cog and a tiny crescent moon at opposite extreme corners, short extremely faint ruler ticks along the very bottom edge. Minimal, practical, warm, quietly magical. Delicate fine graphite strokes and subtle linen fiber, muted coastal blue-gray and small terracotta accents. Visually distinct from floral stationery or golden celestial maps. No flowers, no leaves, no ornate borders, no gold outline, no stars scattered across page, no writing, no numbers, no letters, no labels, no UI widgets, no book photograph, no perspective, no mockup, no shadow outside page, no watermark. Perfect edge-to-edge rectangular background intended to support dense readable UI controls.
