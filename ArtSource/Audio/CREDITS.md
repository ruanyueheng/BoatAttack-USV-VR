# Action audio sources

Only the selected short clips in `Assets/Resources/FishingAudio/SFX/` are runtime assets. Original archives stay here for provenance and rebuilding; the complete source-to-output map and SHA-256 values are in `manifest.json`.

| Source | Author | Declared license | Selected use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | Grass/wood footsteps, stone pickup/contact, mining and wood handling |
| [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) | Kenney | CC0 1.0 | Soft item/cloth handling |
| [Fantozzi's Footsteps](https://opengameart.org/content/fantozzis-footsteps-grasssand-stone) | Fantozzi; single-step selection by qubodup | CC0 | Sand and stone footsteps |
| [Gunshot Sounds](https://opengameart.org/content/gunshot-sounds) | Vincent Sevedge (Tabasco) | CC BY 3.0 archive notice retained; listing also offers CC0 | One SKS shot, cropped, downmixed, filtered and tail attenuated |

The source archives include the publishers' license files where supplied. The Fantozzi archive contains FLAC/OGG variants; the OGG originals are used without another lossy encode. In Unity the short effects decompress to mono PCM.

The gun archive's `sounds/creativecommons.txt` says Copyright (c) 2009 Vincent Sevedge, [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/), while its current download page lists CC0. We retain the archive's author, license link and modification notice as well rather than silently dropping attribution. A copy of these credits is shipped as `Assets/Resources/FishingAudio/Attribution.txt`; keep that notice with any later game distribution.

`ReelInLoop`, `ReelOutLoop` and `FishFlop1–3` are original sound designs generated by `Tools/Art/build_action_audio.py`: ratchet/line-friction loops and soft wet body/tail pats. These are not field recordings, AI audio-service outputs, or extracted audio from Delta Force or another game. The build normalizes generated WAV peaks with headroom and keeps loop seams continuous. They can be replaced by later user-provided Foley while keeping the same resource names.

This root manifest describes the original 34 action effects only. The subsequent nine user-supplied regional music tracks and 16 generated Meowa effects have separate source records under `UserMusic/` and `Meowa/`. See `Docs/REGIONAL_MUSIC_AND_MEOWA.md` for their mapping, provenance and licensing caveats; they are not covered by the CC0/CC BY notices above.
